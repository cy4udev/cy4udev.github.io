Privacy Policy

Banana Pix - AI Image Editing App
Last Updated: December 2024
Your Privacy Matters: This Privacy Policy explains how Banana Pix collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our AI image editing application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

Account Information:
  • Anonymous authentication tokens (if using anonymous login)
  • Authentication data managed by Firebase Auth
  • User preferences and settings

1.2 Images and Content

  • Uploaded Images: Photos you choose to edit using Banana Pix
  • Face Data: Facial images and facial feature data contained within uploaded photos, collected with your explicit consent for AI-powered facial editing features including hair color changes, virtual accessories (earrings, sunglasses, etc.), facial enhancements, and other user-requested facial modifications
  • Generated Images: AI-processed results from your editing sessions
  • Text Prompts: Descriptions you provide for AI image generation
  • Chat History: Your conversation history within the app

1.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • App usage statistics and analytics
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • Performance metrics

1.4 Purchase Information

  • In-app purchase records managed by RevenueCat
  • Credit balance and usage history
  • Subscription status and renewal information
  • Weekly subscription package preferences (Weekly Pro or Weekly Starter)
  • Auto-renewal settings and billing cycles

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Primary Services

  • Image Processing: To perform AI-powered image editing and generation
  • Facial Editing: To process face data for specific editing features such as hair color changes, adding virtual accessories (earrings, sunglasses), facial enhancements, and other facial modifications requested by users
  • Account Management: To maintain your account and preferences
  • Credit System: To track and manage your credit usage and purchases
  • Feature Delivery: To provide app functionality and features

2.2 Improvement and Support

  • Analyze app performance and usage patterns
  • Improve AI model accuracy and performance
  • Provide customer support and troubleshooting
  • Develop new features and enhancements

3. Data Storage and Processing

3.1 Image Processing

Important: Your images are processed by third-party AI services to provide editing capabilities. Images are transmitted securely and processed according to our data protection standards.

3.2 Cloud Storage

  • Firebase Storage: Temporary storage of uploaded images and face data during processing (maximum 8 hours)
  • Firebase Database: Account data, chat history, and app preferences
  • Local Device: App cache and locally generated content

3.3 Data Retention

  • Face Data and Images: All uploaded images containing face data are automatically deleted from Firebase Storage within 8 hours of upload
  • Processed Images: Generated/edited images may be temporarily cached but are not permanently stored on our servers
  • Account Data: Account information is retained while your account is active
  • Analytics Data: Non-personal analytics data may be retained for up to 2 years for service improvement

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Firebase (Google)

  • Purpose: Authentication, database, and cloud functions
  • Data Shared: Account information, app usage data
  • Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy

4.2 RevenueCat

  • Purpose: In-app purchase management and subscription handling
  • Data Shared: Purchase information, user ID
  • Privacy Policy: RevenueCat Privacy Policy

4.3 AI Processing Services

  • Purpose: Image generation and transformation, facial editing features
  • Data Shared: Images (including face data) and editing prompts for processing only
  • Face Data Policy: Face data is NOT shared with third parties for any purpose other than immediate processing
  • Security: All data transmission is encrypted and face data is automatically deleted within 8 hours

5. Data Security

5.1 Encryption

  • All data transmission uses industry-standard encryption (TLS/SSL)
  • Sensitive data is encrypted at rest
  • Images are processed through secure, encrypted channels

5.2 Access Control

  • Limited access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular security audits and monitoring
  • Secure authentication for all system access

6. Your Privacy Rights

6.1 Access and Control

  • View Your Data: Access your account information and usage history
  • Update Information: Modify your account preferences and settings
  • Download Data: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Delete Account: Remove your account and associated data

6.2 Communication Preferences

  • Control notifications and app communications
  • Opt out of analytics data collection (where legally permitted)
  • Manage marketing communications preferences

7. Face Data Collection and Processing

7.1 Face Data Collection

Banana Pix collects facial images and facial feature data contained within photos that you voluntarily upload. This collection occurs only with your explicit consent when you choose to use facial editing features.

7.2 Purpose of Face Data Processing

Face data is used exclusively for:

  • Changing hair color and style
  • Adding virtual accessories (earrings, sunglasses, jewelry, etc.)
  • Facial enhancements and modifications
  • Other user-requested facial editing features

7.3 Face Data Sharing and Storage

Third-Party Sharing: Face data is NOT shared with any third parties for any purpose.

Storage Location: Face data is temporarily stored in Firebase Storage during processing.

Retention Period: All face data is automatically deleted within 8 hours of upload.

7.4 User Control

You maintain full control over your face data. You can delete uploaded images at any time, and all face data is automatically removed from our servers within 8 hours regardless of user action.

8. Children's Privacy

Banana Pix is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete that information immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed and stored in countries other than your own, including the United States. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable laws.

10. Data Breaches

In the unlikely event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will:

  • Notify you promptly as required by law
  • Take immediate steps to secure the affected systems
  • Cooperate with relevant authorities
  • Provide updates on our investigation and remediation efforts

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of significant changes through:

  • In-app notifications
  • Email notifications (if you have provided an email address)
  • Updated version information in the app

12. Subscription Services

12.1 Subscription Packages

Banana Pix offers weekly subscription packages with automatic credit renewal:

Weekly Pro (weekly100sub)

  • Credits: 100 credits per week
  • Usage: ~100 image generations OR ~14 video generations per week
  • Target: Best for regular creators
  • Type: Auto-renewing weekly subscription

Weekly Starter (weekly50sub)

  • Credits: 50 credits per week
  • Usage: ~50 image generations OR ~7 video generations per week
  • Target: Perfect for casual creators
  • Type: Auto-renewing weekly subscription

12.2 Subscription Data Processing

  • Subscription status and package type
  • Weekly credit allocation and usage tracking
  • Auto-renewal preferences and billing information
  • Usage patterns for service optimization
